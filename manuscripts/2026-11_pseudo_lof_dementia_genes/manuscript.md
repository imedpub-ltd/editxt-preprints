# Systematic Landscape of Cryptic-Splice Pseudo-Loss-of-Function Variants, ACMG PVS1 Classification Liabilities, and Allele-Specific Antisense Windows Across Five Autosomal-Dominant Dementia Genes

**Author:** Manuel Menendez-Gonzalez (University of Oviedo, Spain / HUCA / ISPA)  
**Track:** Precision Neurology & Genetics  
**Zenodo Community:** imedpub  
**Repository:** https://github.com/imedpub-ltd/international-archives-of-neurology-neuroscience  

## Abstract
Whether dominant dementia mutations act through simple haploinsufficiency or toxic alteration of gene product directly governs clinical variant interpretation and antisense oligonucleotide therapeutics. Following population-genetic and splicing evidence that authentic PSEN1 null alleles do not cause early-onset Alzheimer's disease (EOAD) whereas the nominal stop-gain PSEN1 c.325A>T (p.K109*) activates a cryptic donor to produce an in-frame deletion (p.K109_L113del), we constructed a systematic five-gene genomic resource comparing three Alzheimer-linked loci (PSEN1, PSEN2, APP), the tauopathy gene MAPT, and the true haploinsufficiency control GRN. Across all 1,092 exhaustive GRCh38 MANE Select stop-gain single-nucleotide variants evaluated with AlphaGenome Variant Impact molecular predictions, predicted splicing-disruption attribution was significantly elevated among cryptic-donor and <=2 bp exon-edge variants compared with interior stop codons (Mann-Whitney U = 42,821.0, p = 9.35e-10). Beyond PSEN1 p.K109*, the atlas prioritizes additional pseudo-LoF candidates including PSEN1 p.R308*, PSEN2 p.K115*, APP p.Y321*, and MAPT p.G725*. Auditing ClinVar exposed diagnostic PVS1 liabilities across 11 pathogenic predicted loss-of-function entries in PSEN1 and PSEN2. Cryo-EM contact analysis (PDB 6IYC) and ESM-2 scoring indicated that p.K109_L113del removes APP-C83 and nicastrin interface contacts within the 5th percentile of embedding perturbation. Finally, nearest-neighbor thermodynamic scanning identified allele-selective 22-mer steric-blocking antisense windows sparing canonical +1/+2 GU donors, led by PSEN1 p.K109* (DeltaDeltaG = +3.63 kcal/mol). Together, these computational annotations establish actionable falsifiable hypotheses for clinical genetic curation and experimental validation.

## Data and Code Availability
All genomic annotations, sequence architectures, and pipeline outputs were derived from publicly accessible resources: Ensembl GRCh38/GRCm39 REST APIs, AlphaGenome Atlas, gnomAD v4.1, NCBI ClinVar, and RCSB PDB (6IYC). All Python pipeline scripts, GeneForge Language catalog specifications, and data tables are packaged in the accompanying Supplementary Archive (Supplementary_Data_and_Code.zip) and open repository.
